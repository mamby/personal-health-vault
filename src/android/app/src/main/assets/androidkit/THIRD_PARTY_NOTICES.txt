## Lucide

The embedded component icons (except the intentional Theme and Language artwork)
are adapted from [Lucide](https://lucide.dev/): `ArrowLeft`, `Check`, `ChevronRight`,
`Circle`, `CircleCheck`, `CircleMinus`, `Code`, `Copy`, `Ellipsis`, `Eye`, `EyeOff`,
`FileText`, `Globe`, `Heart`, `History`, `Info`, `LockKeyhole`, `Mail`, `Mic`, `Pin`,
`PinOff`, `Search`, `Square`, `Trash2`, `Users`, and `X`.

and is represented in Compose vectors and Android vector drawables. The Settings
chevron retains the same artwork in a tight horizontal viewport.
